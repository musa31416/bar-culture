Who We Are

Good drinks. Better company.

Bar Culture is a mobile bar and event staffing company built on 30 years in professional hospitality and 10 years in event bartending and staffing.

We deliver TABC certified bartenders and servers for weddings, festivals, birthdays, bar mitzvahs, concerts, races and more. Every person on our team is personally vetted, so you know exactly who is serving your guests.

From white glove martinis to cowboy hats and cold beer, we match the feel of your event and take care of your guests so you can enjoy it with them.
