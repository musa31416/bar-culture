Who We Are

Good drinks. Better company.

Bar Culture grew out of 30 years in hospitality, the last 10 spent bartending and staffing events all over Texas. Somewhere along the way we noticed the same thing at every great party: people remember how they were treated at the bar.

So that’s what we focus on. We bring TABC certified bartenders and servers to weddings, festivals, birthdays, bar mitzvahs, concerts, races and pretty much anything else worth celebrating. We know every person on our team personally, and we only send people we’d want pouring at our own events.

White glove martinis at a country club, cowboy hats and cold beer at a ranch wedding, we’re comfortable in both. You get to enjoy your own party, and we’ll take care of everyone else.
