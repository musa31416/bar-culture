The things everyone asks before booking a bar. Don't see yours? Just call or text.

Booking & Pricing
How much does it cost?

Our Bartender-Only Service starts at $350 for up to 50 guests. That covers four hours of bar service plus setup and cleanup. Extra hours, extra staff, supplies and travel outside our service area are priced separately. We like to give quotes over the phone so we can get to know your event, so give us a call or send a quote request.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and big events, two to three months ahead is a good idea, and earlier for spring and fall Saturdays since those go fast. For smaller parties a few weeks is often fine. If your date is close, just ask. We will always try to make it work.

Is there a minimum number of hours?

Yes, four hours of service. Setup and cleanup happen on top of that time, so you get the full four hours of pouring. Need more? Additional hours are available on request.

Do you charge for travel?

Travel inside our service area is covered. For events outside it, travel is priced separately and we will include it in your quote up front, so there are no surprises.

How do I lock in my date?

Once you are happy with your quote, a deposit holds your date and the rest is due closer to the event. We will walk you through it when you book.

The Bar & The Drinks
Do you provide the alcohol?

You provide the alcohol, mixers, ice and cups, and we take care of everything behind the bar. Not sure how much to buy? Ask us and we will help you plan it.

What is included?

With our Bartender-Only Service you get professional bartenders, bar setup, four hours of service and cleanup afterward. You provide the alcohol, mixers, ice and cups. If you need extra supplies or staff, just ask and we will price it into your quote.

Can you make a signature cocktail for us?

Absolutely, and it is one of our favorite parts. We can name a drink after the couple, match your colors or recreate a family favorite. Mocktail versions are always on the table too.

Do you offer non-alcoholic drinks?

Yes. Mocktails, lemonades, sodas and infused waters are easy to add so every guest has something good in their hand.

Can you set up outdoors or at my house?

Yes. Backyards, ranches, barns, parks and beaches all work. We just need a flat spot for the bar and, ideally, a power outlet nearby.

Our Team
Are your bartenders certified?

Yes. Our bartenders are TABC certified and trained to serve responsibly. We also personally vet everyone on our team before they ever work one of our events.

How many bartenders will I need?

We plan on one bartender for every 50 guests. If you want craft cocktails or more involved drinks, we go with one for every 25 so nobody waits too long at the bar.

What will your staff wear?

Our standard look is sharp and all black. If you have a theme or dress code, let us know and we will match it.

What happens if a guest has had too much?

Our bartenders are trained to spot it early and handle it quietly and kindly, without making a scene. Keeping your guests safe is part of the job.

Are you insured?

Yes, and we are happy to send proof of insurance to your venue if they need it.

On The Day
When do you arrive?

We usually arrive about an hour before service to set up, so the bar is ready before your first guest walks in.

Do you clean up afterward?

Yes. We break down the bar, clear our area and leave the space the way we found it.

Do you work with my venue and planner?

All the time. We are happy to coordinate directly with your venue, planner or caterer so you have one less thing to manage.
